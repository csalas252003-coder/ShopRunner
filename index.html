ABOUT US

One organized process for your everyday needs

ShopRunner is a personal shopping and distribution service designed for customers who need everyday products but may not have enough time to personally visit the store. Our service connects customers, stores, shoppers, and transportation in one organized process.

From receiving the customer's order up to the final delivery, ShopRunner makes sure that products are properly handled at every stage.

⚑

Our Mission

To provide a convenient, organized, and reliable shopping and distribution service that helps customers save time and effort.

◎

Our Vision

To become a trusted personal shopping and distribution service known for convenience, proper order handling, and dependable customer service.

LOGISTICS

How ShopRunner Works

From customer order to final delivery, every step is organized and checked.

01 — CUSTOMER

Customer

The customer chooses the products they need and provides the necessary order and delivery details.

02 — ONLINE ORDER

Online Order

The customer submits their order through the ShopRunner platform.

03 — ORDER PROCESSING

Order Processing

ShopRunner reviews the requested products, quantities, store location, and delivery information.

04 — STORE / SUPPLIER

Store / Supplier

The assigned ShopRunner shopper goes to the selected store or supplier.

05 — PRODUCT SOURCING

Product Sourcing

The shopper purchases the requested products.

06 — ITEM CHECKING

Item Checking

Each item is checked to make sure the product, quantity, and condition are correct.

07 — PACKING & PREPARATION

Packing & Preparation

The products are organized and packed properly before transportation.

08 — TRANSPORTATION

Transportation

The prepared order is transported from the store to the customer's location.

09 — FINAL DELIVERY

Final Delivery

The customer receives the order together with the receipt and order details.
