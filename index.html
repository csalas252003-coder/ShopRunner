LOGISTICS

Our Distribution Channel

Every order follows a clear process from the customer's request to final delivery.

01

Customer

Customer chooses the products and provides order and delivery details.

02

Online Order

Order is submitted through the ShopRunner platform.

03

Order Processing

We review items, quantities, store location, and delivery details.

04

Store / Supplier

Our assigned shopper goes to the selected store or supplier.

05

Product Sourcing

Requested products are purchased from the selected store.

06

Item Checking

We verify the product, quantity, and condition.

07

Packing & Preparation

Products are organized and packed for transportation.

08

Transportation

The prepared order is transported to the customer's location.

09

Final Delivery

The customer receives the order and receipt details.
