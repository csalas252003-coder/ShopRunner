Our Logistics Process

ShopRunner follows a clear process from the customer's order until final delivery.

01. Customer Order

Customer places an order online.

02. Order Processing

We review items, quantities, store, and delivery details.

03. Product Sourcing

Assigned shopper goes to the selected store or supplier.

04. Item Checking

Each product is checked for correct item, quantity, and condition.

05. Packing

Products are organized and packed for transport.

06. Transportation

The prepared order is transported to the customer.

07. Final Delivery

Customer receives the purchased products and receipt details.

08. Customer Service

We make sure the order is properly completed.

09. Closing

Right products, right customer, right time.
